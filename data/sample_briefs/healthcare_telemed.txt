PROJECT BRIEF: CareConnect Telemedicine & Patient Portal

Client Overview:
CareConnect Health Partners requires a secure, HIPAA-compliant patient consultation and telemedicine platform to connect outpatients with licensed medical specialists.

Core Requirements:
- High-definition WebRTC video and audio telemedicine consultations with waiting room capability.
- Patient onboarding, medical history questionnaires, and insurance card upload/scanning.
- Doctor scheduling engine with appointment booking, automated calendar sync, and cancellation policies.
- Secure in-app messaging between doctors and patients with attachment support for lab results.
- Integration with Epic/Cerner EHR systems via FHIR / HL7 APIs.
- Integrated e-prescriptions routed to national pharmacy networks (Surescripts).
- Payment processing for co-pays and private telehealth consultations.

Compliance & Security:
- Strict HIPAA and HITECH compliance, complete audit logging of every patient data access event.
- Business Associate Agreements (BAA) with all cloud and third-party vendors.
- Zero-knowledge storage of sensitive medical records using AES-256 at rest.

Platforms:
- Responsive Web portal for patients and desktop browser portal for clinic staff.
- Native or React Native mobile applications for iOS and Android.

Estimated Timeline:
- Target delivery within 20 weeks.
