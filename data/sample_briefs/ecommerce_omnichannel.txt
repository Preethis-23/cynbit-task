PROJECT BRIEF: Modern Omnichannel E-Commerce Platform

Client Overview:
RetailDirect Global is seeking to modernize their retail footprint by building a next-generation omnichannel e-commerce platform.

Key Objectives:
We need a robust e-commerce platform with customer login, product catalog management, secure checkout, payments, an admin management dashboard, mobile support (iOS and Android), and personalized AI product recommendations.

Functional Requirements:
1. Customer Accounts & Authentication:
   - Self-service registration, social sign-on, password recovery, profile management.
   - Role-based access control for Shopper, Store Manager, Customer Support, and Super Admin.
2. Product & Inventory Management:
   - Multi-category catalog, SKU management, real-time inventory tracking across regional warehouses.
   - High-resolution media galleries and product variant attributes (color, size, material).
3. Cart & Checkout:
   - Persistent shopping cart across devices, guest checkout option, automated tax and shipping calculations.
4. Payment Processing:
   - Multi-currency support, payment gateway integration (Stripe and PayPal preferred).
   - Saved payment methods adhering to PCI-DSS Level 1 tokenization.
5. AI-Powered Personalization & Recommendations:
   - Real-time product recommendations on home and product pages based on browsing history and collaborative filtering.
   - Intelligent semantic search with auto-suggest and typo tolerance.
6. Omnichannel & Mobile:
   - Responsive web experience optimized for mobile browsers.
   - Dedicated cross-platform mobile apps for iOS and Android.
7. Backoffice Admin & Analytics Dashboard:
   - Order fulfillment workflow, refund processing, inventory alerts, revenue metrics, customer lifetime value reports.

Non-Functional & Operational Requirements:
- Target peak traffic: Support up to 25,000 concurrent active users during seasonal flash sales with sub-250ms page load times.
- Availability: 99.95% uptime target with automated multi-zone failover.
- Security: End-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption, rate limiting against DDoS and scraping bots, GDPR and CCPA compliance.

Third-Party Integrations:
- Payment Gateways: Stripe, PayPal
- Logistics / Shipping: Shippo or EasyPost API
- Transactional Messaging: SendGrid for email notifications, Twilio for order SMS alerts
- Recommendation Model: Cloud-hosted vector database and custom recommendation API

Timeline & Constraints:
- Stated business goal: Beta launch desired in approximately 16 weeks ahead of Q4 holiday shopping.
- Internal budget estimate: Expected in the $80,000 - $120,000 range.
