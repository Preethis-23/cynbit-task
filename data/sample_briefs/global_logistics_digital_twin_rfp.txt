Client: TransFleet Logistics International
Project: Global Fleet Telematics & AI Dispatch Optimization Platform

Overview:
TransFleet Logistics operates 750 Class-8 semi-trucks across 32 distribution hubs. Need cloud-native IoT telematics and AI route dispatch platform to reduce fuel burn by 14% and predict delivery ETAs with 96% accuracy.

Functional Modules:
- FR-01: Real-time MQTT/Kafka telemetry ingestion for 750 trucks (15,000 events/sec).
- FR-02: Machine learning dynamic ETA model (<15min accuracy window).
- FR-03: Dynamic route recalculation engine with toll/fuel optimization.
- FR-04: Offline-first mobile app (iOS/Android) for commercial drivers with HOS logs.
- FR-05: Web operations dispatch console with live geospatial map and geofence alerts.
- FR-06: Predictive maintenance diagnostics for engine and brake health.

Integrations:
- Geotab ELD telematics hardware, SAP ERP billing, Google Maps Fleet API, AWS IoT Core.

Non-Functional & Compliance:
- SOC2 Type II, FMCSA ELD compliance, 99.99% availability, <120ms p99 ingest latency.

Commercial Constraints:
- Target budget: $520,000 - $680,000. Target timeline: 24 weeks.
