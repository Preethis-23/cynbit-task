# Phase 8: Final Demo and Submission Reports

Package the completed prototype for the Cynbit challenge.

## Objective

Deliver a clear demonstration and the three required documents.

## Required reports

### AI Opportunity Report

Cover Cynbit’s public services, the manual pre-sales problem, alternative opportunities, selected opportunity, business value, risks, and measurable success metrics.

### AI/ML Proof-of-Concept Report

Explain the working flow, supported inputs, outputs, screenshots or demo steps, sample results, and known limitations.

### Technical Approach Report

Document architecture, ingestion, prompts, schemas, LLM usage, embeddings, rules, formulas, optional synthetic-data ML, evaluation, security, deployment, and future production training.

## Demo script

1. Start the application.
2. Upload a sample client brief.
3. Show extracted requirements.
4. Show missing information and risks.
5. Show stack, effort, timeline, cost, and margin.
6. Edit an assumption and show recalculation.
7. Generate the proposal.
8. Explain why a human makes the final decision.

## Final quality checklist

- No secrets or private data are included.
- Every estimate shows assumptions.
- Synthetic data is labeled clearly.
- Unsupported claims are removed.
- The app works from a clean setup.
- Reports explain both business value and technical limitations.
